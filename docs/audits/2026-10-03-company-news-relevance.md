# Company news: long-term investment relevance

“昨日动态” selects facts useful for understanding durable earnings, cash flow,
competitive position, customer demand, capital allocation, governance and risk.
It does not fill company rows merely because a ticker was mentioned.

The editorial prompt now distinguishes operating developments from promotional
coverage. Shared deterministic candidate checks exclude cosmetic gift packaging,
trading-flow rankings, consumer instructions, ceremonial awards and incidental
vendor integrations without an operating fact. Checks apply before the per-company
candidate limit and again at publication. Exclusions are archived with reason and
source URL; a wholly uninteresting candidate set is normal editorial silence.

This is not a closed whitelist: unfamiliar events, early products, management
changes, regulatory developments and commercial collaborations remain candidates.
No amount or realized revenue is required. Material contracts, licensing economics,
adoption, distribution or operating results can retain a promotional-looking story.
Existing source, translation and company-identity checks still determine whether a
selected fact can be published. Editorial relevance alone never proves a claim.

Validation covers unrelated-company examples, useful counterexamples, filtering
before the candidate cap, final-gate bypass attempts, and audited silence without an
LLM call. October 1 replay intentionally removes the cosmetic gift-box source while
preserving all ten other source links. October 2/3 production-source replays remain
covered by the complete regression suite. No live email is sent for this change.

Limit: source text and editorial model judgment remain imperfect. These controls
reduce identified low-information classes without promising exhaustive automatic
investment-quality judgments or bypassing original-source verification.
